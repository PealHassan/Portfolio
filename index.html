Affective Computing · 2026

Developing an EEG Dataset (EmoEG) For The Comparative Analysis of Brain Waves Across Different Emotional States

Sadia Sultana, MD. Peal Hassan, Anindro Kumar Roy, Anowar Hossain, Mst Fazilatun Nessa, Nafis Fuad Abir

This study introduces EmoEG, an EEG dataset collected from 40 Bangladeshi university students to support comparative analysis of brain activity across emotional states. Signals were recorded using four scalp electrodes (TP9, AF7, AF8, TP10) and an auxiliary channel, providing a locally collected resource for research into emotion-related neural patterns.
